# Backend Engineer, Payments Platform

Example Pay · Pittsburgh, PA (hybrid) · Full time

## About the team

The Payments Platform team runs the ledger and settlement services behind every transaction at Example Pay: eleven engineers, two product managers, and an on-call rotation that takes reliability personally. We ship weekly and keep a written record of every design decision.

## What you will do

- Design and build services in Go and Java that process settlements for 40 million transactions a month.
- Own the reliability of what you ship: dashboards, alerts, runbooks, and the on-call that goes with them.
- Review designs and code across the platform, and write the design documents for your own work.

## What we are looking for

- Three or more years building backend services in production, in any language.
- Experience with relational databases under load, including schema migrations without downtime.
- Clear written communication; you will explain trade-offs to people outside your team.

## How to apply

Send a résumé and a short note about a system you were responsible for to platform-hiring@example.com with the subject "Backend Engineer, Payments". Applications are reviewed weekly. Please apply by 31 October 2026. We do not accept applications through recruiters for this role.

## Example of a week

Monday: design review for a new settlement path. Tuesday to Thursday: implementation and tests, a deploy on Thursday. Friday: on-call hand-over and a written retrospective of anything that paged.
