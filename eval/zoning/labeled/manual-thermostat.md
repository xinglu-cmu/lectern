# T-240 Smart Thermostat — Installation and User Guide

## Safety information

Turn off power to the heating system at the circuit breaker before removing the old thermostat. Do not install the T-240 on systems above 24 V. If you are unsure which wires carry power, stop and consult an electrician.

## What is in the box

The thermostat unit, a wall plate, two screws, a wire-labelling sheet, and this guide.

## How the T-240 works

The T-240 learns your schedule from the first week of manual adjustments and then proposes a schedule you can accept or edit. It measures temperature and humidity every thirty seconds and reports them to the app over your home Wi-Fi. When the house is empty, detected through your phone's location if you allow it, it lowers the target by a configurable amount.

## Installation

1. Photograph the wiring of your old thermostat before disconnecting anything.
2. Label each wire with the stickers from the sheet, then remove the old unit.
3. Mount the wall plate level and screw it in; feed the wires through the opening.
4. Connect each wire to the matching terminal and press the unit onto the plate until it clicks.
5. Restore power and follow the on-screen setup.

## Troubleshooting

If the screen stays blank after power is restored, check that the C wire is connected; the T-240 needs a common wire for power. If the app cannot find the thermostat, confirm your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the unit.
