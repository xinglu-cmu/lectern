# Request for Proposals: Student Portal Redesign

## 1. Background

The university's student portal was built in 2014 and serves 18,000 students. It has grown into a collection of loosely connected pages that are hard to navigate on mobile devices, and its accessibility has not been audited since launch. Student surveys in 2025 ranked it the most frustrating campus system.

## 2. Scope of Work

The vendor shall deliver a redesigned portal that meets WCAG 2.2 AA, integrates with the existing identity provider, and migrates all current self-service functions. The proposal must include a project plan, a staffing plan with named leads, and a fixed price.

- Vendors must submit proposals by November 3, 2026, 5:00 pm.
- Proposals should not exceed 20 pages, excluding appendices.
- Questions may be submitted until October 20; answers will be posted publicly.

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be scored on technical approach (40 points), relevant experience (30 points) and price (30 points). The university may invite finalists to present.

## 4. Example Timeline

| Phase | Weeks |
|---|---|
| Discovery | 4 |
| Design | 6 |
| Build | 12 |
| Launch | 2 |

## 5. Terms

This request does not commit the university to award a contract. All proposals become public records after award. The university reserves the right to reject any or all proposals.
