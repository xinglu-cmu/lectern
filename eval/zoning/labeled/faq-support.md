# Quillnote — Frequently Asked Questions

Home › Help Center › FAQ

These are the questions our support team answers most often. If yours is not here, search the Help Center or write to support@example.org; we reply within one business day.

## Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited notes on one device, 100 MB of attachments and sharing with up to three people. Paid plans add sync across devices, version history and larger attachments. You can compare plans on the pricing page.

## How do I sync my notes between devices?

Sync is part of the paid plans. Once you are on one, sign in with the same account on each device and your notes appear within a few seconds. Notes written while offline sync the next time the device connects, and if two devices change the same note, both versions are kept and marked so you can merge them.

## How do I reset my password?

1. Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter the email address on your account and check your inbox for a message from us. It can take a minute to arrive; check spam if it does not.
3. Follow the link in the message, choose a new password, and sign in again on every device.

If you no longer have access to that email address, contact support with the account's creation date and the titles of three of your notes so we can verify it is yours.

## Can I export my notes?

Yes, at any time and on any plan. Settings › Export produces a zip file of Markdown files with your attachments alongside them. There is no lock-in: the Markdown is plain and opens in any editor.

## What happens to my notes if I cancel?

Your account moves to the free plan at the end of the billing period. Notes above the free plan's limits stay readable but cannot be edited until you are back under the limits or upgrade again. Nothing is deleted.

## Do you use my notes to train AI models?

No. Your notes are yours. We do not read them except to investigate a reported abuse, and we never use them for training.
