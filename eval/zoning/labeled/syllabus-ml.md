# 10-601 Introduction to Machine Learning — Syllabus, Fall 2026

## Course description

This course covers the core ideas of machine learning: supervised learning with linear and nonlinear models, the bias-variance trade-off, regularization, unsupervised learning, and an introduction to neural networks. Lectures emphasize the reasoning behind each method rather than library calls. By the end you should be able to pick a method for a new problem, train it correctly, and explain why it worked or did not.

## Learning objectives

After this course you will be able to formulate a learning problem, choose and justify a model, estimate its error honestly, and read a methods section of a research paper with understanding.

## Requirements and grading

There are five homework assignments (50%), a midterm (20%) and a final project (30%). Homework is due on Thursdays at 11:59 pm. You must submit through Gradescope; email submissions are not accepted. Each student has three late days for the semester.

## Academic integrity and AI tools

Collaboration on homework is allowed at the level of ideas; written solutions must be your own. You may use AI assistants to explain concepts, but you must disclose any use in a note at the top of your submission, and AI-generated code or text in a submission is a violation of the integrity policy.
