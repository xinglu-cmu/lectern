# Minutes of the Board Meeting — 18 September 2026

Riverside Community Library Association

Present: M. Alvarez (chair), K. Osei, R. Lindqvist, P. Nakamura, T. Brennan (secretary). Apologies: S. Duval. Quorum confirmed.

## 1. Minutes of the previous meeting

The minutes of 21 August were approved without amendment.

## 4. Reading programme

P. Nakamura reported 212 registrations for the autumn reading programme, up from 160 last year. Volunteer numbers are sufficient. No decision required.
