## Notes that stay yours

Quillnote is a note-taking app that keeps every note as a plain Markdown file you can open anywhere. Write on your laptop, read on your phone, share a page with a colleague, and export everything with one click the day you decide to leave. No lock-in, no ads, and we never train models on what you write.

## Why people switch

**Plain files.** Every note is Markdown on disk. Your editor, your scripts and your backups all work on them without us.

**Sync you can trust.** Changes travel between devices in seconds. When two devices edit the same note, we keep both versions and show you the difference instead of silently choosing one.

**Sharing without accounts.** Send a link; the reader does not need to sign up. Revoke it any time.

**Version history.** Every save is kept for a year on paid plans, so a bad edit is never final.
