# Harbor Pay Launches Instant Settlement for Small Merchants

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

PITTSBURGH, PA — 14 October 2026 — Harbor Pay today announced Instant Settlement, a service that moves card revenue into a merchant's bank account within minutes of each sale instead of the industry-standard two business days. The service is available to all merchants in the United States processing under $5 million a year, with no change to existing pricing.

Small merchants carry the cost of settlement delay more than anyone: a café that sells $2,000 on a Saturday waits until Tuesday to pay for Monday's deliveries. Instant Settlement closes that gap. Funds arrive in the merchant's account through the real-time payment rails operated by the major US banks, and the merchant sees each transfer in the same dashboard that shows the sale.

"We built the ledger to settle continuously three years ago and then kept batching the payouts anyway, because that is how everyone did it," said Priya Raman, Chief Product Officer at Harbor Pay. "The surprise was how little we had to change to stop. The hard part was already done."

The rollout follows a six-month pilot with 1,100 merchants in three states. During the pilot, participating merchants reported a 22 percent reduction in short-term borrowing and a measurable drop in overdraft fees. Chargeback rates were unchanged.

Instant Settlement is enabled by default for new merchants and can be switched on by existing merchants from the Payouts page of the dashboard. Transfers above $25,000 continue to settle on the standard schedule while the company completes additional risk review.
