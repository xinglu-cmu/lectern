# Call for Papers — Workshop on Document Security for Language Models (DocSec 2027)

Co-located with the Harbor Conference on Machine Learning · Montréal, 4 May 2027 · docsec2027.example.org

## About the workshop

Language models now read documents that were written by people who know a model will read them. Résumés carry instructions to screening systems, web pages carry text no human sees, and papers under review quote the very attacks they study. DocSec brings together researchers in NLP, security and human-computer interaction to study what happens between a document and the model that consumes it: detection of hidden and model-directed content, provenance, and interfaces that show people what their tools are reading.

## Topics

We invite work on, among other things: detection of hidden text and prompt injection in documents; benchmarks and red-team corpora; functional segmentation and summarisation of documents for model consumption; provenance and watermarking of document content; user studies of oversight interfaces; and measurement of injection in the wild.

## Submissions

Submit papers of up to 8 pages, excluding references and appendices, in the conference's two-column format through the workshop's OpenReview page. Submissions must be anonymised for double-blind review. Short papers of up to 4 pages reporting negative results or work in progress are welcome and will be reviewed to the same standard. Include a limitations section; papers without one will be returned without review. At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the workshop and present the work in person.

## Important dates

Submission deadline: 20 January 2027, 23:59 AoE. Notification: 1 March 2027. Camera-ready: 20 March 2027. Workshop: 4 May 2027.

## Policy on AI assistance

Authors may use language models to polish writing and must disclose doing so in the acknowledgements. Text, results or citations generated by a model without author verification are not permitted, and reviewers may not upload submissions to any external AI service.
